CIPA, the California Invasion of Privacy Act, is a 1967 law against wiretapping and eavesdropping. A CIPA lawsuit claims your website's cookies, pixels, chat widget, or session replay tool let a third party read or record visitors' activity without their consent. Plaintiffs can seek $5,000 per violation, with no proof of harm required.
