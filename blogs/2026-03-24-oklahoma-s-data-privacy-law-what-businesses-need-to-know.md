After nearly a decade of legislative attempts, Oklahoma has joined the growing ranks of states with a comprehensive consumer data privacy law, making it the 20th state with such a law on the books. The Oklahoma Consumer Data Privacy Act (OCDPA) takes effect on January 1, 2027 , giving businesses roughly nine months to prepare.
